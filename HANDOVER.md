# Managing your Denora website

Everything on your website can be changed by you, from any computer. No coding.

---

## How to log in

1. Go to **your-website-address.com/admin**
   (bookmark this — it's the only address you need)
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub**
3. Enter your GitHub username and password

You'll see two sections: **Products** and **Website Text & Contact**.

> Changes take about a minute to appear on the live site. If you don't see them straight away, wait a moment and refresh.

---

## Adding a new piece

1. Click **Products** → **All Products**
2. Scroll to the bottom of the list and click the **+** button
3. Fill in:
   - **Product name** — e.g. *The Rosalind Dress*
   - **Category** — Clothing or Jewellery
   - **Price** — type it how you want it shown, e.g. *₹4,800*
   - **Short description** — one or two lines
   - **Photo** — click to upload from your computer
   - **Sold out?** — leave off for now
4. Click **Save** at the top

That's it. The piece is now on your website.

---

## Marking something as sold out

1. **Products** → **All Products**
2. Find the piece in the list and click it open
3. Turn **Sold out?** on
4. **Save**

The piece stays visible with a "Sold Out" badge, and the order link is hidden.

---

## Changing a price

Same as above — open the piece, edit the **Price** box, click **Save**.

---

## Removing a piece completely

1. **Products** → **All Products**
2. Find the piece and click the **bin / delete icon** next to it
3. **Save**

---

## Changing the order of pieces

Drag pieces up or down in the list, then **Save**. The order in the list is the order on the website.

---

## Changing your contact details or website text

Click **Website Text & Contact**. You can edit:

- **Top banner message** — the dark strip at the very top (leave empty to hide it)
- **Homepage tagline** — the sentence under your name
- **WhatsApp number** — country code + number, digits only (e.g. `919876543210`)
- **Email address**
- **Instagram username** — without the @
- **Clothing / Jewellery section text**
- **Our Story** heading and text

Click **Save** when done.

---

## Photo tips

- **Square photos look best** — the same shape as an Instagram post
- Good light near a window beats any filter
- Keep photos under about 1MB so your site stays fast. If your phone photos are large, run them through [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) first (free, drag and drop)
- A plain background — a white sheet or wooden table — makes pieces stand out

---

## How orders reach you

There's no checkout on the site. When someone clicks **Order This**, it opens a WhatsApp message to you with the piece name already filled in.

So orders arrive as **WhatsApp messages** (or emails). You confirm availability, price and payment directly with the customer.

---

## Things to know

- **Nothing you do in the admin can break the website design.** You're only editing text and photos.
- **Every change is saved in history** — if something goes wrong, it can be undone.
- **Don't share your GitHub password.** That login is what protects your site.

---

## If something goes wrong

Ask the person who set this up for you. Useful things to tell them:
- What you clicked
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened (a screenshot helps a lot)
